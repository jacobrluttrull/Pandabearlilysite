# PandaLily Site Guide

Hey! I made this site as a place to keep everything PandaLily in one spot. This is
just a quick walkthrough of what's on it and how everything works. You don't need to
know anything technical, if you can click around a web page you're already good.

Quick heads up, this is a fan site. I made it because I watch her and thought her
stuff deserved a nicer home. She didn't make it and it's not official, that note is
at the bottom of every page too.

## Signing in

The site asks for a username and password before it lets you in. A little box pops up
that says "PandaLily fan site".

The username is `panda` unless you were given a different one, and the password is
the one that was shared with you.

Your browser should remember it while you're visiting. If that box keeps popping back
up then something got typed wrong, so just close the tab, open the link again and
retype it.

I put a password on it because this is a fan site for someone else's character and I
didn't want it floating around for random people to find or showing up in Google. So
please don't post the password anywhere public!

## Moving around

The menu at the top follows you down the page so you can always get somewhere else.
It has About, Art, Ref Sheet, Credits and Soundboard. Whatever page you're on right
now is highlighted with a little paw print under it.

Clicking "PandaLily" in the top left always brings you back to the home page.

That round button in the top right switches the colors. If you see a moon you're on
the dark green bamboo look, and if you see a sun you're on the light cream one. Click
it to swap. It remembers what you picked, so it'll look the same next time you come
back.

On your phone the menu drops down onto its own line so it isn't all squished. It all
works the same, just tap instead of click.

## The pages

### Home

Her picture, her name and her tagline, and then a list of everywhere you can find
her. Discord, Twitch, YouTube, her VODs channel, Twitter, TikTok, Instagram and
Throne. The Soundboard is up at the top of that list too.

Anything with a ↗ opens in a new tab so you won't lose this page.

### About

This is her profile. There's a big card with her picture and her bio and her lore,
and then some smaller cards next to it.

Vitals is the quick facts, so her species, her role and where she lives. Favorite
Things is what she likes. Hazard Log is what she doesn't like, and yes the warning
triangles are a joke, nothing's actually wrong.

### Art

All the art people have made of her, with the artist credited on every piece. Huge
thanks to all of them!

Click or tap any picture to open it up big. When it's open you get the title, the
caption, and a link to that artist's page if they have one. To close it you can hit
the ✕, press Esc, or just click the dark space around the picture.

If you see a little sprout and "Image coming soon" that just means I haven't added
that one yet.

### Ref Sheet

Her reference sheets. This is the stuff artists actually draw from, so her body,
her expressions, her colors and her outfits.

Each one is laid out as a numbered plate with a caption telling you what you're
looking at. Her old site was pretty rough to read on a phone and this page was the
worst of it, so I rebuilt this one to actually fit your screen properly.

"Reference Pending" means that's a spot I haven't filled in yet.

### Credits

Everybody who helped build her, sorted into four cards.

Live2D Model is the art and the rigging that make her move, and I stuck the model
showcase video at the bottom of that card so you can just watch it right there.
Stream Assets & Screens is her logo and her starting, background and ending screens.
Then there's Emotes and Music.

Each line is the job on the left and the person who did it. If their name is a link
it'll open their page in a new tab. If there's a job with no name yet, that's one I
still need to confirm.

### Soundboard

This is the fun one. It's a grid of her voice clips and I uploaded all of them
myself.

Click or tap a tile to play it. It plays right away and the little bars on the tile
start moving while it's going. Most clips are only a second or two.

Every tile tells you the clip name, how many times it's been played by everyone ever,
and how long it is. Big play counts get shortened, so 1.2k just means around 1,200.

To search, type in the "Search sounds..." box at the top. The grid narrows down while
you type and it tells you how many matched, like "7 of 52 clips". Clear the box to
get them all back.

There's a small download arrow next to each tile if you want to save a clip to your
device.

The page only shows the first 24 clips when it opens so it isn't a giant wall of
tiles right away. Hit "Show all clips" to see the rest. Searching looks through every
single clip either way, so you don't have to expand it first.

You might see a few messages on this page:

"Tuning the chimes..." means the clips are still loading, give it a sec.

"The soundboard is quiet right now" means they didn't load. It's usually just a
connection hiccup, so refresh and try again.

"Nothing rings back for..." means your search didn't match any clip names, try a
shorter word.

"No chimes strung up yet" means I haven't put any clips up.

## Random stuff you might wonder about

If a clip doesn't make any noise, check your phone isn't on silent and that the tab
itself isn't muted. You can right click the tab and hit Unmute site.

New clips just show up on their own. The soundboard grabs the list fresh every time
you open it, so anything I've added since your last visit will already be there. You
don't have to clear anything or refresh anything.

Nothing on here asks you to sign up. There's no accounts, no comments, no email list.
That login at the start is the only thing you ever type in and it's the same one for
everybody.

And if a picture doesn't load it falls back to a placeholder instead of one of those
broken image icons. So "Image coming soon" and "Reference Pending" mean I haven't
added it yet, not that it's broken.
